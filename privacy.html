ThrowsApp Privacy Policy

ThrowsApp does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer.

Event information entered into the app, including competitor names, schools, measurements, and results, is stored locally on the user's device.

When the camera or photo library is used to scan an entry sheet, the image is processed on the device for text recognition. ThrowsApp does not upload scanned images or recognized competitor information to the developer or to an external server.

CSV and PDF result files are created on the device. They are shared only when the user chooses to export them using the iOS or iPadOS sharing system.

Laser measurement information received by ThrowsApp is processed on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

ThrowsApp does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection services.

Purchases of ThrowsApp Pro are processed by Apple through the App Store. ThrowsApp receives purchase entitlement information from Apple but does not receive or store payment card or billing information.
